🎉 Grand Annual Fest Feast & Special Dinner — Coming this Sunday!

On the joyous occasion of the Annual Inter-College Technical & Cultural Fest, Central Mess Halls 1 & 2 will host a Grand Banquet Dinner on Sunday, September 27. Serving timings will be extended from 7:00 PM to 10:30 PM.

Special Feast Menu:
• Starters: Paneer Tikka / Crispy Chicken Kebab with Mint Dip
• Main: Hyderabadi Dum Biryani, Shahi Paneer, Dal Makhani, Garlic Butter Naan & Steamed Rice
• Desserts: Hot Malpua with Rabdi & Chilled Kesar Pista Ice Cream
Hostel residents may bring registered college guests by acquiring a guest coupon from the warden counter. Signed by Chief Warden
Rebate Window
Posted: Sept 18, 2026 Hostel Office

Mess Rebate Applications Open for Mid-Semester Break

Students leaving campus for the upcoming vacation are requested to apply for their mess fee rebate in advance. Under hostel rules, students absent for minimum 3 consecutive days are entitled to 80% daily meal rebate.

  • Last date to submit leave slip: September 24, 2026 (5:00 PM).
  • Submit online or drop approved gate pass at Hostel Office Room 104.
  • Rebate adjustments will reflect in next month's hostel dues statement.
Menu Feedback Result
Posted: Sept 16, 2026 Mess Committee

Tea Station Revisions: Separate Sugar-Free Pot & Herbal Green Tea Added

In response to feedback submitted via MessMate regarding excessive sweetness in morning tea, the caterer has installed separate dispensers:

1. Pot A: Classic Spiced Masala Milk Tea (Pre-sweetened)
2. Pot B: No-Sugar Milk Tea with sugar cubes & jaggery powder placed aside
3. Pot C: Hot Water Dispenser with Green Tea & Lemon Herbal bags
Hostel Regulation
Posted: Sept 12, 2026 Chief Warden

Prohibition: Removal of Mess Utensils to Hostel Rooms

Over 200 stainless steel plates, bowls, and spoons were found abandoned in floor corridors this week. Please be reminded that removing mess utensils outside the dining halls is strictly against hostel disciplinary codes.

Students unwell in their rooms may request sick-diet room delivery by registering with the hostel doctor or resident caretaker.
